How to write a note

The first word is matched to a character or weapon. Typos are corrected automatically.

For a character's talent materials, put talents or a short form such as tal after the name.

The rest of the input is treated as a description or additional information.

Examples: Clori tal, Ayaka talent mora farm, skyharp.
